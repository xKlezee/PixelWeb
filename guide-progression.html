Account progression reference

Levels. Prestige. Legacy.

Pixel's public progression model has three account layers. This guide documents the current caps and access milestones while deliberately leaving reset, reward, XP-curve and persistence details unpublished where present evidence does not establish them.

Reconciled referenceCurrent public capsLifecycle details bounded
01 / Progression spine

Three layers, three different jobs.

The layers are documented by their current role in progression. A familiar Prestige or Legacy name is not used to invent reset, reward or persistence behavior that is not established here.

02 / Main route

Prestige milestones connect the four Worlds.

World access is not a level-only ladder. The first three transitions combine account progression with the relevant World Boss clear.

04 / Evidence boundary

Missing details are left unpublished instead of assumed.

Many games reset levels, grant currencies or apply permanent multipliers when a player Prestiges. This reference does not assume Pixel follows those conventions.

Legacy documentation and design concepts do not override the current reference.

They can identify topics worth revisiting, but they do not establish current XP curves, reset rules, rewards or persistence. Those details remain unpublished until current evidence supports them.

05 / Documentation confidence

The current caps and gates are a reconciled reference.

Level, Prestige and Legacy caps plus the current World/Nexus access milestones are maintained consistently. The deeper lifecycle of Prestige and Legacy remains outside this guide's current evidence scope.
