Pixel Network/Nexus
Endgame

Beyond the world progression lies the Nexus.

The Nexus is the endgame dimension of Pixel Network. Access begins at Prestige IV, remains unlocked once earned, and opens a second progression curve through instance difficulty.

Access

Endgame starts at Prestige IV.

Nexus access is permanent once unlocked. It does not require defeating Viking; Prestige IV is the access condition.

Prestige IV
Instance progression

Difficulty keeps moving after access.

Raphael, Azazel and the dual Abyss/Astral encounter scale through the account's later Prestige and Legacy milestones.

Encounters

Three instances. Four bosses.

The instance structure distinguishes between single-boss and dual-boss encounters. It is part of Nexus progression rather than a separate game mode.

Expanding endgame

The Nexus is a platform, not a finish screen.

Exclusive items and rotating rewards are part of the long-term direction. Raids, progressive dungeons and The Crucible of Legends remain planned content and are kept separate from what is currently playable.
