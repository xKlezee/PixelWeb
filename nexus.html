Pixel Network/Nexus
Endgame

The access layer for Pixel's endgame.

The Nexus unlocks after reaching Prestige IV and defeating Viking, the final required World Boss in the current world route. That first unlock is permanent, while later instance milestones continue through Prestige VII, Legacy I and Legacy II.

Access

Endgame opens at Prestige IV + Viking.

Both conditions are required once: reach Prestige IV and defeat Viking. After that first clear, Nexus access stays unlocked permanently on the account.

Prestige IV + defeat Viking
What changes

Reaching the Nexus starts a second progression curve.

The four-world route ends with Viking. From there, account milestones determine which instance difficulties become available instead of asking the player to clear another world gate.

Permanent unlock

The final world gate is earned once.

Prestige IV proves account progression; Viking proves completion of Winter's final combat milestone. Together they permanently unlock Nexus access.

Instance ladder

Difficulty becomes the next gate.

Raphael and Azazel begin at Easy as Nexus opens. Abyss + Astral joins later, and the full ladder continues into Legacy progression.

Encounters

Three instances. Four bosses. Three difficulties.

The current endgame ladder contains two single-boss encounters and one dual-boss encounter, each with a defined account milestone path.

Expanding endgame

The destination can grow without changing its identity.

Future Nexus content extends the same endgame destination rather than splitting the account into another unrelated mode. These additions belong to Pixel's future endgame direction rather than the current playable foundation.

Current foundation

Final world clear + instance progression.

The current foundation begins when Prestige IV and the Viking clear permanently unlock Nexus, followed by the defined difficulty ladder tied to later Prestige and Legacy milestones.
