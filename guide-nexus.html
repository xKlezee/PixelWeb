Nexus & Instances.

Nexus is Pixel Network's separate endgame destination. This reference documents the reconciled encounter catalogue and the current account-based access and difficulty model without extending those facts into boss behavior that is not covered here.

Reconciled reference · September 2026— Instance encounters— individual bosses
This guide has a deliberate scope.

It owns Nexus access, encounter structure and difficulty milestones. Encounter-specific phase behavior belongs to separate verification and is not inferred from the catalogue alone.

01 / Access model

One account threshold defines Nexus access.

Access is separate from the later milestones that expand Instance difficulty.

Nexus access is defined at —. Reaching that account milestone makes the destination permanently available under the current public model.

Winter remains the final current World, but its final World Boss is not an additional Nexus access requirement. World progression and Nexus remain separate content layers.

Viking is not the Nexus gate.

The Nexus access definition is the — account threshold; Viking closes Winter's World Boss arc instead.
