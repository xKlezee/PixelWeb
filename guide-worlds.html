03 / Boundary

Winter ends the World route. Nexus is not another World.

The website deliberately keeps the two concepts separate because they use different progression models.

Winter is the final current World. Viking closes Winter's World Boss arc. The following endgame destination is Nexus, but its access is an account milestone rather than another World transition.

Nexus access is documented as a permanent — unlock. Defeating Viking is not an additional Nexus access requirement. Instance difficulty then expands later through account progression.
