Pixel Network/Systems
Systems

A progression stack, not a feature list.

Pixel Network is built around a small set of systems that change access, equipment and long-term goals. Supporting systems add depth around that progression instead of competing with it for attention.

Signature systems

The systems that define the account.

These are the mechanics that most directly determine what a player can reach, collect and challenge over time.

01 / PROGRESSION300

Levels, Prestige and Legacy

The account curve extends through 300 levels, 10 Prestige tiers and 4 Legacy tiers. Prestige opens world and endgame access; Legacy continues the difficulty curve after Prestige.

300 levels · 10 Prestige · 4 Legacy

System map

Progression changes what comes next.

The main journey is not a scripted quest chain. Account milestones, world access and selected boss clears create the structure naturally.

LevelsBuild the account toward the current level cap of 300.
PrestigePrestige becomes an access condition for later worlds and endgame milestones.
World gatesBeholder, HollowKeeper, Eldric and Viking combine with Prestige to close each progression world.
NexusPrestige IV + Viking completes the world route and permanently unlocks Nexus access.

Quests

Quests exist in two current contexts.

Pixel does not currently use a universal questline to drive the four-world progression. Quests instead belong to systems where they have a clear purpose.

BattlePass

Activity-driven seasonal progression.

BattlePass uses its own quests as a secondary track. It rewards continued activity without replacing permanent account progression.

Dedicated questsSecondary progressionSeparate from world gates
Skyblock

Questing outside the PvP path.

Skyblock has quests tied to island progression, giving players who prefer building and cooperation another structured way to advance.

Island-linked questsPersonal progressionCo-op compatible
Supporting systems

Depth around the core, not beside it.

These systems reinforce equipment, collection, activity or social play. They matter because of what they add to the main account, not because the website gives each of them equal billing.

Equipment

Blacksmith & Forge

A dedicated place for equipment improvement and preparation outside the immediate combat loop.

Item history

StatTrack

Selected equipment can retain performance history, giving individual items context beyond their base statistics.

Creature progression

Bestiary

Repeated encounters can contribute to a broader record of what the player has fought and progressed through.

Seasonal activity

BattlePass

BattlePass adds a quest-driven progression track without replacing the permanent level, Prestige and Legacy structure.

Social play

Parties

Players can organize around shared activity and encounters without turning the network into isolated queues.

Rewards

Crates & Keys

Reward containers sit beside the progression model rather than defining who can access its core systems.
