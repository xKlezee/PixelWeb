Systems

The mechanics that carry progression forward.

Pixel Network does not treat every feature as equally important. A small set of systems defines the long-term journey; the rest exists to support those decisions, collections and milestones.

The systems players build around.

Supporting systems

Depth around the core, not beside it.

Supporting systems are useful because they reinforce equipment, activity, collection or social play. They are not presented as separate headline features when they do not need to be.

Equipment

Blacksmith & Forge

Equipment progression gains a dedicated place for improvement and preparation outside the immediate combat loop.

Item history

StatTrack

Selected equipment can retain performance history, giving individual items context beyond their base statistics.

World activity

Bestiary

Creature-focused progression gives repeated encounters a clearer place within long-term play.

Seasonal progression

BattlePass

BattlePass uses its own quests as a secondary progression track, rewarding continued activity without replacing the permanent account journey.

Social play

Parties

Players can organize around shared activity and encounters without turning the network into isolated queues.

Rewards

Crates & Keys

Reward containers sit beside the core progression rather than defining it.
