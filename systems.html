Pixel Network/Systems
System architecture

One account.
Systems that build on it.

Pixel is not a stack of disconnected features. Levels create the base account, Prestige changes access, world gates prove progression, Nexus shifts the model to endgame difficulties, and collections and equipment grow around that same route.

Progression spine

The account changes what the player can reach.

This is the structural layer underneath Worlds and Nexus. It is milestone-driven rather than a universal quest chain.

Core systems

Collection, equipment and resources reinforce the route.

Not every mechanic needs its own card. These three systems carry most of the useful supporting information around permanent progression.

Quest tracks

Two current contexts. Neither drives the four-world route.

Questing has a defined place in Pixel without becoming a universal progression script.

BattlePass

Seasonal activity track.

BattlePass uses its own quests as a secondary progression layer. It rewards continued activity while permanent levels, Prestige, boss gates and Legacy remain separate.

Dedicated questsSecondary progressionSeasonal activity
BattlePass quests do not replace the world progression structure.
Skyblock

Island-specific progression.

Skyblock has its own quests tied to the player's island, alongside building, upgrades, collaborative play, visits, personal storage and personal vaults.

Island questsOwn progressionCo-op compatible
Skyblock is another way to play inside Pixel Network, not a universal questline for the four worlds.
