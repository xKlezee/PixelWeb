System architecture

One account.
Systems with different jobs.

The important part is not how many mechanics Pixel has, but how they fit together. This page separates the systems that shape account progression from those that support collection, equipment, quests and social play.

Progression spine

The account changes what becomes available.

Levels, Prestige and Legacy form the account progression spine. Current caps and access milestones have a dedicated evidence-qualified reference; reset, reward and persistence semantics are not inferred on this overview.
