Owners

Two disciplines shaping the same network.

Klezee leads gameplay, systems and world design. PxlMads leads the visual language across textures, interfaces, bosses and mobs. Both own the direction of Pixel Network.

Ownership

Direction stays close to the work.

Gameplay and presentation are developed side by side. Decisions move directly between world design, mechanics, code, interfaces and visual assets instead of being separated into disconnected departments.

01 / OwnerGame & Technical Direction

Klezee

World Design · Mechanics · Programming · Level Design

Klezee defines how Pixel Network plays: the structure of its worlds, progression, mechanics, technical systems and level design. The work spans from gameplay planning through implementation, keeping the playable experience consistent from one system to the next.

World DesignGame MechanicsProgrammingLevel DesignProgression
Leads the playable layer — worlds, systems, progression and the technical behavior behind them.
02 / OwnerVisual Direction

PxlMads

Textures · Menus · Bosses · Mobs · Visual Presentation

PxlMads defines the visual language of Pixel Network. That includes the textures players recognize, the menus they navigate, and the visual direction behind bosses, mobs and the elements that give each system its own identity.

TexturesMenus & UIBoss DesignMob DesignVisual Direction

How the work connects

Different disciplines. Shared quality bar.

The split is clear, but the work is not isolated. Mechanics influence presentation; visual decisions influence readability, encounters and level design.

Klezee

World & level design

Terrain, structures and spaces are designed around movement, progression, encounters and player understanding.

Klezee

Systems & programming

Custom mechanics and progression behavior are built to support the intended game loop rather than exist as isolated features.

PxlMads

Visual assets and menus establish a consistent language across items, navigation and player-facing systems.

PxlMads

Bosses & mobs

Creature presentation, visual identity and encounter-facing assets give progression milestones a recognizable character.
