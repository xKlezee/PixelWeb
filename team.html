Pixel Network/Team
The people behind Pixel

Small team. Direct ownership.

Pixel Network is not presented as a fictional studio with a dozen generic departments. The public team page reflects the people actually directing and engineering the project.

Owner / Direction

Klezee

Leads the product direction, visual identity, progression model and long-term priorities of Pixel Network. The role spans game design, project decisions and the overall player experience.

Engineering

Pau101

Works on the technical systems and custom mechanics that turn the project direction into functioning gameplay. Engineering is treated as part of the design process rather than a separate implementation layer.

Working model

Fewer handoffs. More continuity.

A smaller team makes it possible to keep gameplay, technical constraints and visual direction in the same conversation.

Direction

One product model

Features are evaluated against the same progression and player-experience goals instead of being added independently.

Engineering

Custom where it matters

Technical work focuses on mechanics and behavior that materially change the experience rather than custom code for its own sake.

World design

Gameplay in physical space

Worlds and structures are treated as part of onboarding, progression and atmosphere, not merely decorative maps.

Iteration

Audit and refine

Existing systems can be revisited when performance, usability or balance no longer match the intended experience.

Community

Feedback without design-by-committee

Player feedback informs problems and friction while the project keeps a coherent product direction.

Presentation

Show the work accurately

The public site demonstrates real project depth without inventing roles, fake scale or inaccessible internal details.
