FAQ

Common questions.

Short answers for information that should not be scattered across unrelated pages.

What is Pixel Network?

Pixel Network is an independent Minecraft Java Edition network built around long-term RPG-style progression, Worlds, collection systems, Skyblock and Nexus endgame content.

Where should a new player begin?

Use the Play action to enter the server, then start with the Progression Guide. The Guide is the maintained player-facing reference for progression and mechanics.

Where are the current rules?

The canonical player-facing rules are published on the Rules page. Older screenshots, posts or archived documentation should not be treated as a replacement for the current ruleset.

Who runs Pixel Network?

The currently published ownership and responsibilities are listed under Staff Team. Private or unpublished staff membership is not inferred or displayed.

Is the Store required to progress?

No. Pixel Network's core progression remains available through play. Store purchases are optional support and may provide the products or benefits described on the official Store.

Where can I see recent changes?

Use the Changelog for player-facing release history and the Guide for maintained mechanics and progression information.
