Economy reference

Currencies.

Pixel Network uses more than one currency. Keeping them separate matters: Coins support the broad gameplay economy, Pixels appear in wallet and store-facing flows, and Nexus Points belong to the Nexus currency layer.

CoinsPixelsNexus Points
Gameplay economy

Coins

The general-purpose economy used across multiple current gameplay systems.

Role

Main gameplay currency

Coins are used by the broader gameplay economy, including progression-facing systems and Skyblock economy features.

Where you see them

Gameplay & progression

Current systems can reward, spend, deposit or withdraw Coins depending on the feature. Exact prices and reward amounts belong to the system that owns them.

Wallet & store

Pixels

A distinct Pixel Network currency used by wallet/store-oriented player flows.

Role

Separate from Coins

Pixels have their own balance and should not be treated as another name for Coins. Store-facing systems can use a Pixels balance for purchases.

Reference rule

No invented conversion rate

This guide does not publish a Coins↔Pixels conversion or unverified acquisition rate. Any exact cost belongs to the feature or store surface where it is actually used.

Endgame currency

Nexus Points

The currency associated with the Nexus layer of Pixel Network.

Nexus Points are their own currency.

They are documented separately from Coins and Pixels. Exact earning routes, costs and exchange behavior are only published when the corresponding Nexus economy rules are verified for the current server build.

Documentation boundary

Names are stable; values belong to their systems.

The currency guide explains what each balance represents. It deliberately avoids carrying old prices, reward rates or thresholds forward without current evidence.
