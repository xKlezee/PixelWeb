Overworld, Pirate Kingdom, Nether and Winter are the complete current world route. Every transition inside that route pairs account progression with a required boss clear, while Kraken remains optional.

The route

See the four-world progression before reading the rules.

The visual layer is data-driven. Each card represents one current world; endgame begins only after this route is complete.

Gate logic

Every world transition combines Prestige + combat.

The same rule carries through the four-world journey: reach the account milestone and defeat the boss that closes the current stage.

World transitions

The first three gates move between worlds.

Advancing to Pirate Kingdom, Nether and Winter requires both the corresponding Prestige milestone and the progression boss of the previous world.

After the world route

Winter is where world progression ends.

Prestige IV and Viking complete the fourth world. Nexus then unlocks as a separate endgame layer rather than a fifth world.

Encounter roles

Five encounters across four worlds.

Beholder, HollowKeeper, Eldric and Viking close progression stages. Kraken remains a secondary Pirate Kingdom encounter and gates nothing.
