Pixel Network/Worlds
World progression

Four worlds. One route forward.

Every world changes the pressure without resetting the player. Mines, World Bosses and Prestige requirements build one continuous route toward permanent Nexus access.

The route

See the world, the boss and the gate together.

The progression card for each world is generated from one shared data source, including its media, boss role and next unlock requirement.

Gate logic

Boss clears matter only where the route says they do.

The first three transitions combine Prestige with a named combat milestone. Winter deliberately breaks that pattern when the Nexus opens.

Required transitions

Prestige + progression boss.

The route uses both account progression and a required World Boss clear before opening Pirate Kingdom, Nether and Winter.

Prestige I + Beholder → Pirate KingdomPrestige II + HollowKeeper → NetherPrestige III + Eldric → Winter
Nexus exception

Prestige IV is enough.

Viking remains Winter's World Boss, but it is not part of the Nexus access condition. Once Prestige IV is reached, Nexus access unlocks permanently.

Prestige IV → NexusViking remains a World BossNo Viking clear required
