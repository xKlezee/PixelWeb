Pixel Network/Worlds
World progression

The current worlds are stages, not separate modes.

Overworld, Pirate Kingdom, Nether and Winter form the launch progression. Prestige and boss clears determine access, while the structure remains open to additional worlds as Pixel grows.

Current route

Four worlds before endgame.

The route below uses the current verified unlock requirements. Nexus access is a separate Prestige IV milestone and does not require defeating Viking.

01 / Foundation

The Overworld

The first stage establishes mining, equipment, economy and combat before progression starts asking for boss clears and Prestige.

  • 9 mines
  • World Boss: Beholder
  • No entry requirement
  • Pirate Kingdom requires Prestige I + Beholder
02 / Expansion

Pirate Kingdom

Pirate Kingdom introduces the first explicit World Boss gate while keeping room for optional content inside the same stage.

  • 6 mines
  • HollowKeeper gates progression
  • Kraken is optional and does not gate content
  • Nether requires Prestige II + HollowKeeper
03 / Pressure

The Nether

The third stage sits deeper in Prestige progression and makes Eldric the clear completion requirement for opening Winter.

  • 6 mines
  • World Boss: Eldric
  • Unlocked at Prestige II after HollowKeeper
  • Winter requires Prestige III + Eldric
04 / Late progression

Winter

Winter is the final current world before the Nexus, but it is not designed as the permanent limit of Pixel's world structure.

  • 6 mines
  • World Boss: Viking
  • Unlocked at Prestige III after Eldric
  • Nexus unlocks at Prestige IV; Viking is not required
