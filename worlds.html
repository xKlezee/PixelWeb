Pixel Network/Worlds
World progression

Four environments, each with a different role.

Worlds define more than atmosphere. Moving forward changes access, reward quality and the level of risk the player is expected to handle.

01 / Foundation

The Overworld

The first stage establishes the rules of Pixel Network. Players learn how mining, economy and combat connect before higher-risk systems are introduced.

  • Starter mining and progression
  • Early equipment and PvP decisions
  • Handcrafted terrain and structures
  • First major progression gate
02 / Expansion

Pirate Kingdom

The second stage raises the reward ceiling and expects stronger preparation. Familiar systems remain, but the cost of poor decisions becomes more noticeable.

  • Pirate-themed equipment and drops
  • Higher-risk PvP spaces
  • Distinct encounters and NPCs
  • Progression toward late-game content
03 / Pressure

The Nether

Late progression brings greater exposure to PvP and stronger reward pools. The Nether is where preparation, equipment value and risk become harder to separate.

  • Rare talisman and elixir opportunities
  • Elite hostile encounters
  • Late-game reward pools
  • Greater open-PvP pressure
04 / Advanced progression

Winter

The final world layer before endgame gives experienced players a new environment for systems they already understand, with higher expectations around preparation and execution.

  • Advanced resource and combat pressure
  • Distinct late-game reward opportunities
  • Higher-value progression decisions
  • Bridge into endgame systems

World access is part of the progression.

Gameplay shows how equipment, economy, combat and bosses support each transition.
