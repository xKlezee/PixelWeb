World progression

Four worlds.
One route forward.

Overworld, Pirate Kingdom, Nether and Winter are stages of one account journey. Prestige defines readiness, selected bosses prove progression, and Nexus changes the model once endgame access begins.

The route

See the progression before reading the rules.

The visual layer is data-driven. Images are requested only as their cards approach the viewport, and every gate shown below comes from Pixel's public progression structure.

Gate logic

Prestige and bosses are separate requirements.

The first three transitions combine account progression with a named boss clear. Nexus deliberately breaks that pattern.

World transitions

Three gates combine Prestige + combat.

Advancing to Pirate Kingdom, Nether and Winter requires both the corresponding Prestige milestone and the progression boss of the previous world.

Prestige I + BeholderPrestige II + HollowKeeperPrestige III + Eldric
Nexus exception

Prestige IV is the access condition.

Viking remains Winter's World Boss, but it does not gate Nexus. Once Prestige IV is reached, Nexus access unlocks permanently.

Prestige IV → NexusViking is not requiredAccess persists
Encounter roles

Five encounters. Four different progression jobs.

Bosses are not presented as interchangeable trophies: required clears, optional content and the Winter exception are kept explicit.
