Overworld, Pirate Kingdom, Nether and Winter are the complete current World route. The first three transitions combine an account milestone with the required World Boss clear, while Kraken remains optional. Nexus access is handled separately through the account progression model.

Gate logic

The first three World transitions combine account progression + combat.

Moving from Overworld to Pirate Kingdom, Pirate Kingdom to Nether and Nether to Winter requires both the relevant account milestone and the progression boss of the previous World.
