World progression

Worlds are progression stages, not separate modes.

Overworld, Pirate Kingdom, Nether and Winter form the current launch route. Prestige controls readiness, selected World Boss clears prove progression, and the Nexus begins under a different rule.

Current route

Four worlds before endgame access.

The first three transitions require both Prestige and a specific World Boss clear. The transition from Winter to Nexus is intentionally different: Prestige IV is enough.

Gate logic

Prestige and bosses do different jobs.

Prestige marks account progression. World Boss clears are added only where a world transition explicitly calls for one.

World transitions

Three gates combine Prestige + boss completion.

The route asks for both account progression and a named combat milestone before Pirate Kingdom, Nether and Winter become available.

Prestige I + Beholder → Pirate KingdomPrestige II + HollowKeeper → NetherPrestige III + Eldric → Winter
Nexus exception

Prestige IV opens endgame directly.

Winter still has Viking as its World Boss, but Viking is not part of the Nexus access condition. Reaching Prestige IV is the verified requirement.

Prestige IV → NexusViking remains a World BossViking does not gate Nexus
World Boss index

Not every encounter has the same progression role.

The distinction matters: some bosses are required clears, one is optional, and one exists in the final current world without blocking Nexus access.

Overworld · Required clear

Beholder

Beholder is the combat requirement paired with Prestige I for opening Pirate Kingdom.

Pirate Kingdom · Required clear

HollowKeeper

HollowKeeper is the principal Pirate Kingdom World Boss and pairs with Prestige II to open Nether.

Pirate Kingdom · Optional

Kraken

Kraken is a secondary thematic encounter. It adds content to Pirate Kingdom but does not gate any progression.

Nether · Required clear

Eldric

Eldric pairs with Prestige III to open Winter and is the last boss-clear requirement in the current world route.

Winter · Not a Nexus gate

Viking

Viking remains Winter's World Boss, but the Nexus unlock is based on Prestige IV rather than a Viking clear.

World roles

Each stage changes the pressure, not the identity of the game.

The worlds share one account progression. Their purpose is to advance requirements, resources and combat milestones rather than reset the player into a new mode.

01 / Foundation

The Overworld

The opening stage establishes the shared language of mining, equipment, economy and combat before later access begins requiring both account progression and boss completion.

  • 9 mines
  • World Boss: Beholder
  • No entry requirement
  • Pirate Kingdom: Prestige I + defeat Beholder
02 / Expansion

Pirate Kingdom

Pirate Kingdom is the first stage where a required World Boss and an optional encounter coexist. HollowKeeper controls progression; Kraken adds content without becoming a mandatory checkpoint.

  • 6 mines
  • Progression World Boss: HollowKeeper
  • Optional encounter: Kraken
  • Nether: Prestige II + defeat HollowKeeper
03 / Pressure

The Nether

The Nether pushes the same account deeper into Prestige progression and gives Eldric a clear structural role: the last required boss clear before the final current world.

  • 6 mines
  • Progression World Boss: Eldric
  • Entry: Prestige II + defeat HollowKeeper
  • Winter: Prestige III + defeat Eldric
04 / Late progression

Winter

Winter is the last world in the current launch route, not the permanent end of Pixel's world structure. Its World Boss and the Nexus unlock deliberately remain separate milestones.

  • 6 mines
  • World Boss: Viking
  • Entry: Prestige III + defeat Eldric
  • Nexus: Prestige IV · no Viking clear required
