Overworld, Pirate Kingdom, Nether and Winter are the complete current world route. The first three transitions combine an account milestone with the required World Boss clear, while Kraken remains optional. Nexus access is handled separately at Prestige IV.

The route

See the four-world progression before reading the rules.

The visual layer is data-driven. Each card represents one current world; Nexus is a separate endgame destination rather than a fifth world.

Gate logic

The first three world transitions combine Prestige + combat.

Moving from Overworld to Pirate Kingdom, Pirate Kingdom to Nether and Nether to Winter requires both the corresponding Prestige milestone and the progression boss of the previous world.

World transitions

Three gates move between the four worlds.

After the world route

Winter and Nexus use separate conditions.

Viking is Winter's final World Boss, but it does not gate Nexus. Nexus becomes available permanently at Prestige IV as a separate endgame layer.

Encounter roles

Five encounters across four worlds.

Beholder, HollowKeeper and Eldric gate later worlds. Viking is Winter's final World Boss. Kraken remains a secondary Pirate Kingdom encounter and gates nothing.

Endgame

Nexus becomes available at Prestige IV.

Nexus is a separate permanent endgame unlock with its own Instance difficulty ladder. Viking is not required for access.
