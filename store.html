How it works

What you buy and how you support Pixel move together.

Buying Store content also contributes to the account's lifetime support progression. When the account reaches a rank milestone, the rank upgrades automatically and future support continues from the same cumulative history.

1 · ChoosePurchase available Store content.
2 · AccumulateThe purchase contributes to lifetime support.

4 · ContinueFuture purchases build on the same support total.
5 · Keep playingThe normal gameplay progression remains available through play.

Store content

Supporting Pixel does not require buying a rank as a standalone product.

Purchases across available Store content can contribute to the same support path. Current public categories include cosmetics, keys and decoration.

Cosmetics

Visual identity.

Optional visual content can support the network while still contributing to lifetime support progression.

Keys

Reward access.

Keys sit inside the wider reward economy rather than replacing the long-term progression structure.

Decoration

Personal expression.

Decorative purchases provide another way to support Pixel without making the Store only about rank packages.

Progression & purchases

Faster progression is possible; exclusive progression is not the goal.

Store purchases can accelerate parts of progression, but Pixel's core progression and power systems remain obtainable through play. The Store is designed as a support and convenience layer around the game, not a separate route that replaces playing it.
