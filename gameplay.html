Pixel Network/Gameplay
Main progression

Progression happens naturally through the systems you use.

Pixel does not force a scripted sequence on new players. Levels, equipment, mining, combat, collections and world access create the journey as the account develops.

Account progression

A readable structure without a fake checklist.

The long-term model has three layers. The exact pace changes as players advance, while the public structure stays easy to understand.

What advances the account

Different activities, one main progression.

The point is not to make every activity identical. Mining, PvP, equipment and collections each create different decisions while contributing to the same account.

Mining

27 mines across the current worlds

Mining starts with nine Overworld mines and continues through six mines in each later world, giving resource progression a physical route.

Combat

PvP has progression context

Equipment, preparation and resource value affect what players are willing to risk rather than turning combat into a disconnected queue.

Equipment

Builds gain specialization

Custom enchantments, forging and item history create reasons to care about how equipment develops over time.

Collection

Talismans follow advancement

The Talisman Codex gives world progression a collectible layer with a defined set of 36 progression talismans.

Worlds

Access becomes a milestone

Prestige and specific boss clears unlock later worlds, turning location itself into part of account progression.

Endgame

Nexus begins at Prestige IV

Prestige IV permanently unlocks Nexus and opens Raphael Easy and Azazel Easy. Viking remains Winter's final World Boss, but it is not required for Nexus access.

Two progression styles

Main progression is not the only way to spend time in Pixel.

Players who prefer building and cooperation can progress through Skyblock without the website pretending that island play and world progression are the same system.

Main journey

Worlds, bosses, mining and PvP.

Advance through four current worlds, increase Prestige and defeat the bosses that gate later worlds. At Prestige IV, Nexus opens as a permanent endgame layer with its own Instance difficulty ladder.
