Pixel Network/Skyblock
Skyblock

Your island. Your pace. Shared when you want it.

Skyblock gives players a personal progression path built around owning, improving and sharing an island. It sits alongside the main world journey for players who prefer building and cooperation over PvP.

Personal progression

A place that keeps becoming more yours.

Your island has its own progression. The focus is deliberately simple: build it, improve it and decide how much of that experience you want to share with other players.

Your island

Build and improve over time.

The island is persistent personal space with its own progression. Building is not treated as a temporary side activity.

Progress without PvP

A parallel route through Pixel.

Skyblock has its own quests for island-focused progression. Quest content also exists in the BattlePass; neither should be confused with a universal questline driving the four-world progression.

Objectives

Skyblock quests

Skyblock quests give island-focused players structured goals tied directly to their own progression path. BattlePass quests exist separately as part of seasonal progression.
