Pixel Network/Skyblock
Skyblock

Your island. Built to persist.

Skyblock is Pixel's personal island-progression route: create a lasting space, improve it over time, use island-focused economy systems and follow objectives designed for this mode. Collaboration controls are being completed separately and are not presented as fully available yet.

Island foundation

A persistent place with its own progression.

Your island is a lasting gameplay space that can be created, restored and improved over time. Its progression is built around developing that space rather than treating Skyblock as a temporary activity.

Your island

Build, save and improve.

Your island persists as part of the mode, so building and progression can continue across sessions instead of restarting as a disposable activity.
